Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains what we collect when you use Mark Talks Health, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send purchase receipts.
  • Your purchases, so we know which programs to unlock for you.
  • What you enter in your app (for example your intake answers, symptoms, test results, start date and daily tracking) and your course progress and quiz results, so your plan and progress are there on every device.
  • Email list sign-ups, if you join our list.

How we use it

Only to run the programs for you: signing you in, unlocking what you bought, saving your plan and progress, answering your questions and, if you joined the list, telling you about new programs. We don't sell your information, and we don't use your health information for advertising.

Who processes it

Your account and app data are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Payments are handled by Stripe, which receives your payment details directly. Videos may be served by a video host such as YouTube, Vimeo or Wistia.

On your device

We keep your sign-in on your device so you stay signed in (you can turn this off with "Remember me"). The app also keeps a copy of your plan on your device so it works quickly.

YouTube and Google

Mark Talks Health uses YouTube API Services to publish and manage videos on our own YouTube channel. This happens inside a private admin area of this website that only our own team can sign in to. When our team connects our YouTube channel, we sign in with Google, and Google shares an access token with us so we can upload our own videos and thumbnails, read view, like and comment counts and analytics for our own videos, and read and reply to comments on our own videos.

  • By using these features you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google's handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • What we store from YouTube: the sign-in token for our own channel, our own videos' details and statistics, and comments left on our own videos (the commenter's public channel name and comment text), so we can see results and reply. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Cookies and device storage: our admin keeps its sign-in on the device it is used on. We do not place cookies on your device for YouTube features.
  • Removing access: you can revoke this website's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services).
  • Deleting data: YouTube data we store is refreshed regularly and is deleted within 30 days once access is revoked, or sooner on request. If you commented on one of our videos and want that comment removed from our records, email us and we'll delete it within 7 days.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing us. You can leave the email list at any time.
